How to play

Pick up loads around the valley and deliver them. Take the bumps gently or your cargo ends up on the road.

W A S D / arrows drive · Space handbrake · Shift fire boost · H horn · C camera · T time of day · Y weather · R back to road · Esc show / hide your errand

Villagers wait at the stops with a little sign saying where they're going. Roll up slowly and they hop in the back (two at a time), then follow the compass (it turns their colour) to their place: drop them off to get paid and fill every gas tank. Big bumps bounce them out, and they don't like being bowled over!

Everyone else just out for a walk gets scooped into the back if you drive into them, and each one is a wanted star (up to five). Police cars that touch you rescue a rider. Drive into the lake with riders aboard and the police give up, your riders swim for the shore, and your boost fills right up (no riders? dive fully under to lose them).

Controller: Left stick steer · RT/A gas · LT/B brake · X handbrake · RB boost · Y horn · D-pad camera · D-pad back to road · Select time of day · Start pause

How to fill the boost
  • Bump into trees to knock oranges down. 5 oranges = 1 segment
  • Every completed errand fills 1 segment
  • Roll up slowly next to sheep so they hop in the back. 3 sheep = all 3 segments full! Big bumps bounce them out
  • Your tank holds 1 segment at first, and grows to 3 after your first errand
